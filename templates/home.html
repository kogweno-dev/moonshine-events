How it works, from first call to final clean-up

Simple, predictable and written down, so you always know what happens next.

  1. Free consultation

    Call, WhatsApp or visit us. We listen to the vision, the date and the honest budget.

  2. Concept and quotation

    You receive a colour scheme and an itemised quote, usually within 48 hours.

  3. Planning and setup

    We visit the site, lock the suppliers, build a run-sheet and set up ahead of time.

  4. Event day and teardown

    Our coordinator runs the programme while you host. We clear the site the same day.
